Everything you need to stay on track

Read-aloud assistant

Hear each medication's name, dosage, and instructions spoken out loud, in your chosen language.

Multi-language

Switch the whole app between English, Spanish, French, and Mandarin with one tap.

Pill counter and low-stock alerts

Counts drop automatically when you mark a dose taken, and you're warned when only a few days remain.

Caregiver alerts

Optionally notify a trusted person by email or SMS if a critical dose isn't taken within 60 minutes.

Pill visualizer

Pick a pill's shape, color, and imprint to double-check it matches the medication you expect.

Food and timing guide

Clear badges like "Take with food" or "Avoid dairy" on every medication card.

Adherence streak

Build a daily streak of 100% adherence and stay motivated.

Label scanner and AI help

Photograph a bottle label to auto-fill details, check interactions, or ask the AI assistant a question.

MedSnap is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice. AI results can be wrong, so always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions.
